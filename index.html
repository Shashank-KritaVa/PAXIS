What is PAXIS? PAXIS is an intelligent safety system that uses ground cameras, computer vision software, and a civilian quadcopter drone to watch crowded areas like railway stations, schools, stadium exits, air raid shelters, and refugee camps. It predicts stampedes before they start and detects fallen individuals or casualties so rescue teams can respond immediately.

SECTION 01 — HUMANITARIAN SCOPE

EXECUTIVE ABSTRACT

PAXIS PREVENTATIVE PARADIGM
💡 IN SIMPLE WORDS

In times of fire, sudden alarms, or war evacuations, most injuries happen because scared people rush to the same narrow exit at once. PAXIS predicts crowding panic beforehand and includes an AI counting feature that spots fallen or injured persons on the ground, allowing rescue personnel to navigate directly to victims without delay.

During military conflicts, severe industrial disasters, or regional crises, civilians gather in dense clusters at safe zones, border evacuation checkpoints, air-raid shelters, refugee transit camps, and disaster relief centers. Similarly, high-density public venues—such as railway stations, shopping malls, religious gatherings, stadiums, and school emergency assembly points—experience acute congestion during sudden alarms.

In these high-stress situations, instinctual panic triggers immediate mass egress. Casualties frequently stem not directly from the initial threat, but from uncontrolled crush pressures, bottlenecks, and unobserved fallen individuals trapped underfoot.

Traditional CCTV networks record events passively but fail to provide predictive situational awareness or automated casualty localization due to physical blind spots and operator exhaustion during chaotic incidents.

PAXIS bridges this gap by combining Ground Vision Monitoring with a civilian Aerial Surveillance Module (ASM). Computer vision algorithms calculate crowd density metrics, velocity vectors, running anomalies, and fallen human body locations in real time—triggering immediate edge-level alerts via Arduino edge hardware while the drone offers real-time aerial verification.

War & Peace Relevance

Peace is defined by the active protection of human life. By detecting crush hazards and locating fallen individuals in humanitarian corridors, PAXIS provides a non-offensive technological shield for rapid rescue operations.

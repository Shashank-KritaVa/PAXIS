            <p>

              During military conflicts, severe industrial disasters, or regional crises, civilians gather in dense clusters at safe zones, border evacuation checkpoints, air-raid shelters, refugee transit camps, and disaster relief centers. Similarly, high-density public venues—such as railway stations, shopping malls, religious gatherings, stadiums, and school emergency assembly points—experience acute congestion during sudden alarms.

            </p>

            <p>

              In these high-stress situations, instinctual panic triggers immediate mass egress. Casualties frequently stem not directly from the initial threat, but from uncontrolled crush pressures and bottlenecks occurring within seconds.

            </p>

            <p>

              Traditional CCTV networks record events passively but fail to provide predictive situational awareness due to physical blind spots and human operator fatigue during chaotic incidents.

            </p>

            <p>

              <strong class="text-white">PAXIS</strong> bridges this gap by combining Ground Vision Monitoring with a civilian Aerial Surveillance Module (ASM). Computer vision algorithms calculate crowd density metrics, velocity vectors, and running anomalies in real time, triggering immediate edge-level alerts via Arduino edge hardware while the drone offers real-time aerial verification.

            </p>

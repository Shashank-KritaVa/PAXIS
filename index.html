07 / WAR OR PEACE

When movement
matters most.

During emergencies, conflict-related incidents, evacuations, or sudden crowd panic, people may move rapidly toward exits or perceived safe zones. Rapid, uncontrolled movement can increase crowd density and create dangerous conditions.

PAXIS is intended to add an early-warning layer by observing crowd conditions and identifying abnormal changes in movement or density. The drone adds an elevated viewpoint when ground-level cameras have limited visibility.

MISSION NOTE

“Awareness can create time for clearer decisions.”

PAXIS does not claim to predict attacks, identify terrorists, guarantee prevention of stampedes, or take autonomous action.
